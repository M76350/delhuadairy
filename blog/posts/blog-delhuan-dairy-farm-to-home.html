Delhuan Dairy Ka Farm-to-Home Quality Promise

Delhuan, Rohtas se fresh dairy products ko customer tak pahunchane mein quality, hygiene aur trust hamare core principles hain.

Hamari process healthy animal care, clean milking, batch-level quality checks, hygienic packing aur planned delivery par based hai. Har stage ka purpose customer ko fresh aur responsible dairy experience dena hai.

Milk testing aur transparency

Fat, SNF aur purity screening ke saath handling records ko bhi importance milti hai. Quality ko sirf marketing line nahi, daily farm practice banana hamara goal hai.

Customer ke saath rishta

Feedback, delivery timing aur product information ko clear rakhkar local families ke liye dependable service build ki ja sakti hai. Aapke questions hamare quality improvement ka hissa hain.
