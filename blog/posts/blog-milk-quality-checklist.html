Milk Quality Checklist: Farm Se Customer Tak

Quality ko ek single test nahi, poori farm-to-home chain ke checklist ke roop mein manage karein.

Milking se pehle animal aur udder hygiene check karein. Collection par sample, fat/SNF aur basic smell observation note karein. Accepted milk ko covered container mein jaldi chill karein.

Packing aur delivery

Clean food-grade packaging, batch time, delivery route aur refrigerator guidance customer ko provide karein. Complaint aaye to batch aur delivery record trace karein.

Checklist: Clean source, tested batch, fast chilling, hygienic pack, timely delivery aur clear feedback channel.

Is system ko daily repeat karne se consistency improve hoti hai aur customer confidence banta hai.
