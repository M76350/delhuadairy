Milk Chilling Kyon Zaroori Hai: Cold Chain Guide

Milking ke baad time aur temperature dono control karna milk quality ke liye important hai.

Fresh milk warm environment mein microbial growth ke liye sensitive hota hai. Isliye collection ke baad covered food-grade container aur rapid chilling process use karein.

Cold chain ke stages

Farm par milk handling, collection point, transport aur customer refrigerator: har stage par delay kam rakhein. Temperature log ya simple time record se process monitor kiya ja sakta hai.

Ghar par storage

Milk ko refrigerator ke cold section mein covered rakhein, door shelf par nahi. Smell ya appearance unusual lage to use consume karne ke bajay discard aur supplier ko inform karein.

Quality mantra: Clean milk plus fast chilling equals better freshness, lekin chilling poor hygiene ka replacement nahi hai.
