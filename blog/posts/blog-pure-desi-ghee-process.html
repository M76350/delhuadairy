Pure Desi Ghee Banane Ki Traditional Process

Bilona-style process mein curd se makkhan aur phir slow heating se ghee banaya jaata hai.

Traditional process ki shuruaat quality milk se hoti hai. Milk ko set karke dahi banaya jaata hai, dahi ko churn karke makkhan nikala jaata hai aur makkhan ko controlled heat par cook kiya jaata hai.

Quality signs

Achhe ghee ka aroma clean hota hai, texture consistent hota hai aur moisture low rakhne ke liye proper cooking zaroori hoti hai. Burnt smell ya excess sediment poor process ka sign ho sakta hai.

Storage

Ghee ko dry, clean aur airtight glass jar mein sunlight se door rakhein. Wet spoon use na karein.

Traditional method ka value process transparency, ingredients aur hygiene se banta hai; sirf label se nahi.
