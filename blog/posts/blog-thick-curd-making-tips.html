Ghar Jaisa Thick Dahi Jamane Ke Tips

Consistent dahi ke liye clean starter, sahi temperature aur undisturbed setting zaroori hai.

Milk ko boil karke lukewarm temperature tak laayein. Bahut hot milk starter culture ko damage kar sakta hai aur thanda milk setting slow kar sakta hai.

Starter aur setting

Fresh, active dahi ka small portion use karein. Container ko cover karke warm, stable jagah par rakhein aur set hone tak hilayein nahi.

Texture improve karne ke tips

  • Full-fat milk se naturally richer texture mil sakta hai.
  • Utensils clean aur dry rakhein.
  • Set hone ke baad refrigerator mein rakhein.

Temperature aur starter ki quantity ko apne weather ke hisaab se adjust karke best result paaya ja sakta hai.
