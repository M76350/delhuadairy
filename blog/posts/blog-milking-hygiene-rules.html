Milking Hygiene: Saaf Doodh Ke Liye 10 Rules

Milk quality animal ke health ke saath milking area, hands, utensils aur chilling hygiene par bhi depend karti hai.

Milking se pehle hands wash karein, udder ko clean water se saaf karke dry karein aur saaf, food-grade utensils use karein. Wet surfaces bacteria growth ko badha sakti hain.

Daily checklist

  1. Milking area ko dry aur dust-free rakhein.
  2. Har animal ke liye clean cloth use karein.
  3. First few streams ko separate check karein.
  4. Milk ko filter karke covered container mein rakhein.
  5. Utensils ko wash, sanitize aur air-dry karein.
  6. Animals ko unnecessary stress na dein.
  7. Sick animal ka milk alag rakhein.
  8. Milk ko jaldi chill karein.
  9. Transport container ko daily inspect karein.
  10. Cleaning aur testing records maintain karein.

Hygiene routine consistent ho to contamination risk kam hota hai aur customer ko better quality milti hai.
