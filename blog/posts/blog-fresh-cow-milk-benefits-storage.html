Fresh Cow Milk Ke Fayde aur Sahi Storage Method

Fresh milk ka nutrition aur taste tabhi achha rehta hai jab milking se kitchen tak hygiene aur temperature control bana rahe.

Cow milk protein, calcium aur kai nutrients ka source hai. Iske benefits individual diet, age aur health needs par depend karte hain, isliye balanced diet ka hissa banakar use karein.

Delivery ke baad kya karein

Milk ko clean, covered container mein rakhein aur jitni jaldi ho sake refrigerator mein chill karein. Raw milk use karne wale parivaar ko apne doctor ki advice aur local food safety guidance follow karni chahiye.

Storage mistakes

  • Room temperature par lambi der tak milk na chhodein.
  • Wet ya unclean ladle container mein na daalein.
  • Purane aur naye milk ko bina cleaning ke mix na karein.

Freshness ek chain hai: clean milking, rapid chilling, safe transport aur ghar par proper storage. In sab par dhyan dene se quality maintain hoti hai.
