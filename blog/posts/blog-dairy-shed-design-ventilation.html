Dairy Shed Design: Ventilation aur Drainage

Achha shed animals ko heat, rain aur dampness se bachata hai aur daily cleaning ko easy banata hai.

Shed ka orientation, roof height, cross ventilation, shade aur non-slippery floor pehle plan karein. Drainage slope aisa ho ki urine aur wash water standing na rahe.

Animal comfort

Har animal ko enough resting space, clean bedding aur easy water access dein. Garmi mein airflow aur shade stress kam karte hain.

Cleaning workflow

Feed area aur dung passage ko alag rakhna hygiene improve karta hai. Daily cleaning ke liye water point aur safe waste collection zone plan karein.

Design principle: Shed sundar se zyada washable, ventilated aur animal-friendly hona chahiye.
