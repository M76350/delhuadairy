Small Dairy Farm Business Plan: Budget aur Income

Chhote dairy farm ko profitable banane ke liye realistic budget, reliable customers aur daily discipline zaroori hai.

Business plan mein animals ki purchase ke alawa shed repair, fodder, medicines, labour, electricity, transport aur packaging ka kharcha jodein. Emergency fund ko alag rakhein.

Income ka realistic estimate

Milk yield har animal, season aur feed ke saath badalti hai. Isliye maximum yield ke bajay conservative average se sales calculate karein. Ghee, paneer ya dahi jaise products tabhi add karein jab cold storage aur demand clear ho.

Customer retention

Fixed delivery time, honest quantity, clean packaging aur quick feedback response repeat orders badhate hain. Local families, tea shops aur small restaurants alag customer segments ho sakte hain.

Practical rule: Har mahine sales ke saath per-litre cost aur wastage percentage bhi review karein.

Small dairy farm ki strength personal service aur quality transparency hoti hai. Growth ko records ke basis par step-by-step rakhein.
