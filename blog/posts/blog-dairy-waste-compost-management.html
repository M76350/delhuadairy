Dairy Farm Waste Management aur Gobar Se Compost

Planned waste management se farm clean rehta hai aur manure ko useful resource mein badla ja sakta hai.

Gobar, bedding aur wash water ko alag handling stream mein rakhein. Drainage ko open water source se door design karein aur flies ya smell ko ignore na karein.

Compost basics

Organic material ko dry matter, moisture aur airflow ke saath controlled pile mein compost kiya ja sakta hai. Ready compost ko farm soil mein use karne se pehle local agriculture guidance lein.

Biogas, compost aur tree plantation jaise options farm ke size aur resources ke hisaab se choose karein. Clean surroundings animal health ko bhi support karte hain.
