Bihar Mein Local Dairy Farm Chunne Ke 7 Tarike

Local farm choose karte waqt sirf price nahi, quality process aur service transparency bhi dekhein.

Farm ka location, animal care, milking hygiene, testing process, chilling, packaging aur delivery time ke baare mein poochhein. Visit possible ho to clean water, shed aur utensils observe karein.

Customer checklist

  • Product aur price clearly written hain?
  • Quality concern par contact person available hai?
  • Delivery container clean aur covered hai?
  • Farm apne claims ko records se support karta hai?

Reliable local dairy woh hai jo quality ke saath honest communication aur consistent service bhi de.
