Milk Testing Kaise Hoti Hai: Fat, SNF aur Purity Guide

Achhe doodh ki quality sirf taste se nahi, balki regular testing aur hygienic handling se samjhi jaati hai.

Milk testing ka purpose consumer ko consistent quality dena hai. Collection ke samay sample ko saaf container mein lekar fat, SNF, temperature aur smell jaise basic parameters dekhe jaate hain.

Fat aur SNF ka matlab

Fat doodh ki richness batata hai, jabki SNF mein protein, lactose aur minerals aate hain. Reading ko samajhte waqt sample ka temperature aur testing instrument ki calibration zaroor check karein.

Purity check ka responsible tareeka

Lactometer ya rapid test sirf screening ke liye hote hain. Kisi bhi doubtful sample ke liye accredited laboratory test sabse bharosemand hota hai. Paani, starch ya detergent jaisi milawat ke claims ko bina lab confirmation ke final na maanein.

Delhuan Dairy practice: Doodh ko test karne ke saath saaf milking, jaldi chilling aur batch records bhi quality system ka hissa hone chahiye.

Customer ko kya poochna chahiye

  • Milk collection ke baad kitni jaldi chilling hoti hai?
  • Testing instruments ki cleaning aur calibration ka record hai?
  • Storage aur delivery temperature kaise maintain hota hai?

Transparent process se farmer aur customer dono ka trust banta hai. Quality testing ko ek single number nahi, poori farm-to-home chain ke roop mein dekhein.
