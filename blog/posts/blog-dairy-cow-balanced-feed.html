Dairy Cows Ke Liye Balanced Feed Chart

Feed plan cow ki age, body condition, milk yield, pregnancy aur season ke hisaab se tay hona chahiye.

Balanced ration mein green fodder, dry fodder, energy source, protein source, minerals aur clean water shamil hote hain. Ek hi feed sabhi animals ke liye sahi nahi hoti.

Feed routine kaise banayein

Daily quantity ko din mein alag meals mein baantna aur feed ko achanak badalne se bachna useful hai. Fodder ko dry, mould-free aur hygienically stored rakhein.

Water aur minerals

Milk production ke liye water availability bahut important hai. Mineral mixture ka use local veterinarian ya nutritionist ke recommendation ke hisaab se karein.

Important: Feed chart ko animal-specific banane ke liye qualified livestock professional se consultation lein.

Achha feed plan animal health, milk consistency aur farm cost control teeno ko improve karta hai.
