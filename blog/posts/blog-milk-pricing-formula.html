Milk Pricing Kaise Tay Karein: Transparent Formula

Fair price farmer ki quality, business cost aur customer value ko balance karti hai.

Pricing banate waqt feed, animal care, labour, testing, chilling, packaging, delivery aur wastage ko per-litre cost mein include karein. Sirf competitor ka rate copy karna sustainable nahi hota.

Quality-linked pricing

Fat aur SNF jaise measurable parameters ko transparent formula se joda ja sakta hai. Customer ko quantity, delivery frequency aur packaging ka clear breakup dein.

Best practice: Price changes ko pehle se communicate karein aur invoices ya monthly summaries maintain karein.

Honest pricing se farmer ko fair return aur customer ko predictable service milti hai.
