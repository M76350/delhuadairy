Dairy Farm Records Kaise Maintain Karein

Accurate records guesswork ko kam karte hain aur dairy farm ke decisions ko measurable banate hain.

Rozana animal-wise milk yield, feed quantity, health observation, medicine, breeding aur sales record karein. Date aur responsible person ka naam likhne se entries traceable rehti hain.

Monthly review

Mahine ke end par per-animal yield, feed cost, medical expense, wastage aur customer complaints compare karein. Trends se underperforming area identify hota hai.

Simple notebook, spreadsheet ya dairy app mein se wahi system choose karein jise team consistently maintain kar sake.
