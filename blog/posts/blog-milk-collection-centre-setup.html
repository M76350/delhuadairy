Milk Collection

Milk Collection Centre Kaise Set Up Karein

Collection centre par fast testing, clean handling aur transparent records se farmer aur customer dono ka trust banta hai.

Centre ke liye clean floor, drainage, ventilation, weighing scale, sample containers, testing equipment aur reliable power backup plan karein. Entry aur milk handling ka flow simple rakhein.

Daily process

Milk receive karte waqt quantity note karein, sample test karein, rejected milk ko alag rakhein aur accepted milk ko jaldi chill karein. Har batch ka time aur operator record karein.

Transparency

Farmers ko test parameters aur payment formula clearly samjhaayein. Equipment ki cleaning, calibration aur maintenance schedule display karna useful hai.

Focus: Collection centre ka goal volume badhana nahi, safe aur traceable milk chain banana hai.
