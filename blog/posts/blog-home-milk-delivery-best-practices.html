Home Milk Delivery Business Ke Liye Best Practices

Reliable home delivery ka foundation timing, clean packaging aur clear customer communication hai.

Customers ko delivery window, quantity, price aur holiday policy pehle batayein. Route ko locality-wise plan karne se fuel aur time bachta hai.

Packaging aur records

Food-grade reusable containers ko wash aur sanitize karein. Subscription, payment, pause request aur feedback ka digital ya paper record maintain karein.

Service recovery

Delay ya quality concern ho to customer ko jaldi inform karein, batch trace karein aur documented solution dein. Trust perfect delivery se nahi, responsible response se bhi banta hai.
