Healthy Cow Ke Signs: Farm Observation Checklist

Daily observation se cow ke health changes jaldi identify karne mein madad milti hai.

Healthy cow alert hoti hai, normal appetite aur rumination rakhti hai, comfortably chalti hai aur clean eyes, coat aur breathing pattern dikhati hai. Milk yield ko bhi routine ke saath compare karein.

Daily checks

  • Food aur water intake.
  • Rumination aur dung consistency.
  • Body temperature ya illness signs.
  • Udder health aur milk appearance.
  • Movement aur resting behaviour.

Kab vet ko bulaayein

Achanak milk drop, fever, lameness, breathing difficulty, abnormal discharge ya khana chhodna serious sign ho sakta hai. Qualified veterinarian se contact karein.
