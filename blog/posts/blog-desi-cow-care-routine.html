Desi Cow Care: Rozana Health Routine

Healthy cattle ke liye regular routine, clean housing aur early observation sabse effective foundation hain.

Subah shed ko inspect karke water trough, floor aur bedding clean karein. Cow ka appetite, rumination, movement aur body condition daily observe karna kisi problem ko jaldi pakadne mein madad karta hai.

Feed aur rest

Fresh fodder, dry fodder, mineral supplement aur clean water ka balanced plan banayein. Feed change dheere karein aur vet ya nutrition expert ki salah lein.

Health red flags

  • Milk yield mein achanak girawat.
  • Khane ya rumination mein kami.
  • Lameness, unusual discharge ya fever.

Red flags par home remedy ke bajay qualified veterinarian ko bulaana safer hai. Vaccination aur deworming records updated rakhein.
