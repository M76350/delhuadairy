Fresh Paneer Ki Quality Kaise Check Karein

Fresh paneer ka texture, aroma aur storage condition purchase se pehle aur baad dono check karein.

Achha paneer clean dairy aroma, soft but firm texture aur even colour wala hota hai. Excessively sour smell, slimy surface ya unusual colour quality concern ho sakta hai.

Storage aur use

Paneer ko covered container mein refrigerate karein aur clean knife use karein. Room temperature par lambi der na rakhein. Pack par date aur storage instruction follow karein.

Trusted source

Fresh paneer choose karte waqt preparation date, milk source, hygiene aur delivery timing ke baare mein poochhein. Local product ka advantage tabhi hai jab process transparent ho.
