Pure Milk Ki Pehchan: Ghar Par Kaun Se Tests Karein

Ghar par kiye gaye checks ek initial indication dete hain; final confirmation ke liye laboratory testing zaroori hoti hai.

Pure milk ki quality dekhne ke liye sabse pehle source aur handling par dhyan dein. Doodh ka smell natural ho, container saaf ho aur delivery ke baad use turant thanda kiya jaaye.

Simple observation checks

Rang, smell, texture aur heating ke baad ka behaviour note karein. Bahut zyada watery texture ya unusual smell warning sign ho sakta hai, lekin in observations se akela milawat prove nahi hoti.

Reliable testing kyon zaroori hai

Milawat ke alag substances ke liye alag tests hote hain. Isliye internet par milne wale har home test ko definitive na samjhein. Trusted dairy se test report aur process ke baare mein poochna behtar hai.

Yaad rakhein: Doodh ko ubaalna hygiene ke liye useful hai, par ubaalna har chemical adulterant ko remove nahi karta.

Safe buying checklist

  • Sealed ya clean reusable container use karein.
  • Delivery ka time aur cold storage process jaanen.
  • Suspicious sample ko consume na karke lab advice lein.

Pure milk ka matlab sirf ek test result nahi, balki responsible farming, clean milking aur transparent supply chain hai.
