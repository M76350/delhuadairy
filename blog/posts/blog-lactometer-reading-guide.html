Milk Testing

Lactometer Reading Ko Sahi Tarah Kaise Samjhein

Lactometer milk ki density ka screening tool hai, purity ka akela proof nahi.

Sample ko clean cylinder mein bharke lactometer ko gently float karein. Temperature reading ko instrument ke reference ke saath compare karein, kyunki temperature density ko affect karta hai.

Common mistakes

Foam, dirty instrument, wrong eye level, incomplete mixing aur temperature ignore karna reading ko inaccurate bana sakta hai. Har test ke baad instrument wash aur dry karein.

Reading ka use

Lactometer reading ko fat aur SNF test, smell observation aur farm records ke saath interpret karein. Suspicious result par laboratory confirmation lein.

Responsible testing mein tool ki limitation samajhna utna hi important hai jitna reading note karna.
