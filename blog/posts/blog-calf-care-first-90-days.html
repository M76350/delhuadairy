Calf Care

Calf Care Ke Pehle 90 Din Kyon Important Hain

Early nutrition, warmth, hygiene aur timely veterinary care calf ke long-term health foundation hain.

Birth ke baad colostrum timing aur quantity ke liye veterinarian ke guidance ko priority dein. Calf ko clean, dry aur draft-free area mein rakhein.

Nutrition aur hygiene

Feeding utensils ko wash karke dry rakhein aur feed changes gradual rakhein. Fresh water aur age-appropriate starter feed ke liye local livestock expert se plan banayein.

Records

Birth date, weight, vaccination, deworming aur illness notes maintain karein. Early warning signs par delay na karein.

Important: Calf care ka exact schedule region aur animal condition ke hisaab se vet se confirm karein.
