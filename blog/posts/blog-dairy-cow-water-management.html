Dairy Cows Ke Liye Clean Water Management

Clean, accessible water animal health, feed intake aur milk production ke liye basic requirement hai.

Water trough ko daily clean karein aur algae, dirt ya stagnant water ko remove karein. Source water ki quality ko periodic testing se verify karein.

Garmi mein hydration

Shade, airflow aur multiple water points se heat stress reduce hota hai. Water restriction ke bajay vet-guided routine banayein.

Water ka record aur cleaning schedule simple register mein rakhein. Clean water dairy hygiene ka sabse basic step hai.
