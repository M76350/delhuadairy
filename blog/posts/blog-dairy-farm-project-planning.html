Dairy Project

Dairy Farm Project Shuru Karne Ki Complete Planning

Ek sustainable dairy project animals kharidne se pehle planning, records aur market understanding se shuru hota hai.

Project ka size local demand, available land, water aur aapke management time ke hisaab se tay karein. Pehle pilot scale par operations samajhna risk ko kam karta hai.

Project ke mukhya hisse

Shed, fodder storage, clean water, milking area, waste drainage, veterinary support aur customer route ko plan mein include karein. Har item ka capital aur monthly operating cost alag likhein.

Animals aur feed selection

Healthy animals ko records aur veterinary examination ke saath kharidein. Feed plan mein green fodder, dry fodder, mineral mixture aur seasonal changes ko jagah dein.

Business tip: Pehle buyers aur delivery route identify karein, phir herd size badhayein. Unsold milk cash flow ko turant prabhavit karta hai.

Records se project control

  • Daily milk yield aur fat reading note karein.
  • Feed, medicine, labour aur electricity ka expense track karein.
  • Monthly profit ko total sales nahi, net cost ke baad dekhein.

Achha dairy project quality, animal welfare aur customer trust teenon ko saath lekar chalta hai.
