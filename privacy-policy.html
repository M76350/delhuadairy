Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Delhuan Dairy aapki privacy ka samman karta hai. Yeh policy batati hai ki website par di gayi information ka use kaise kiya jaata hai.

Contact form ya phone/WhatsApp enquiry mein aapka naam, phone number, email aur message mil sakta hai. Hum sirf enquiry ka jawab dene aur dairy service provide karne ke liye iska use karte hain.

Information ka sharing

Hum personal information ko sell nahi karte. Delivery ya legal requirement ke liye zaroori ho tabhi trusted service provider ya authority ke saath information share ki ja sakti hai.

Cookies aur analytics

Website basic session storage ka use announcement banner preference ke liye kar sakti hai. Third-party images ya fonts ki apni privacy policies ho sakti hain.

Data safety aur contact

Reasonable care ke baad bhi internet transmission completely risk-free nahi hota. Privacy concern ke liye Sampark page se message bhejein.
