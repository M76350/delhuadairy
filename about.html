Namaste, Main Hoon

Anil kumar singh

Main Anil kumar singh, Delhuan Village, Rohtas, Bihar ka rehne wala hoon. Delhuan ko yahan Delhua ya Deluaon bhi kaha jata hai. Baalpan se hi gaay-bhains ke saath bada hua, aur tab se ek sapna tha — ek aisa dairy farm banana jo poore ilaake ko shuddh aur taaza doodh de sake.

Saal 2009 mein, sirf 5 desi gaayein se shuruat ki. Koi badi capital nahi thi, koi brand nahi tha — bas tha lagan, mehnat aur ek pakka iraada ki quality se kabhi compromise nahi karenge.

Aaj, 15+ saalon ki mehnat ke baad, hamare paas 150+ desi gaayein hain, 500+ litres rozana ka utpaadan hai, aur 1200+ parivaaron ka vishwas hai.

Hamara pata: Delhuan Village, Chitaon Panchayat, Indour Post Office, Dinara Thana, Kochas Prakhand, Rohtas, Bihar — 802213, India.

"Doodh mein milawat sirf dhokha nahi, woh ek parivaar ki sehat ke saath khilwad hai. Aur main yeh kabhi nahi karne dunga."

— Anil kumar singh

Mission & Vision

Hamara Mission

Har ghar ko shuddh, taaza aur poshan se bharpoor A2 dairy utpaad pradan karna — bina milawat, bina chemical ke. Seedha farm se seedha ghar tak.

Hamara Vision

Bihar ka sabse bharosemand aur jaana-maana dairy brand banna — jahan quality aur transparency ek dusre ka doosra naam ho. 2030 tak 5000+ parivaaron ki seva karna.
